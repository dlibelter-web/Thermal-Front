THERMAL FRONT

Squeeze the FSR to fly up, release to sink. Turn the pot to change your temperature: low = FROST, middle = WATER, high = FIRE. Your ship fires on its own.

Chrome or Edge required. Close the Arduino Serial Monitor first.
Keyboard mode: ↑/↓ ship height, 1 2 3 frost/water/fire (or hold Q/E to sweep the pot), Space select, ←/→ menus, F fullscreen, Esc or P pause (hold Esc to leave fullscreen), D debug overlay + cheats.
Sound: plays on the computer automatically when no Arduino is connected. With an Arduino, press M to also play sounds on the computer (handy if the piezo is quiet).
